We designed and built this Desk Extender as part of a team-based product development project. Our goal was to solve a real problem many students face every day: having desks that are too small to comfortably hold laptops, notebooks, and materials.
 We went through multiple iteration cycles, testing, evaluating, and refining our designs until we created a portable, durable, and
 classroom-friendly extension that could attach to nearly any standard desk surface. The final product performed well during our design review and was recognized by all three judges as one of the strongest concepts due to its practicality and portability.

![Shaded rendering of earlier version]({{ "/assets/images/DeskExtenderCAD2.jpg" | relative_url }}){: .inline-image-r style="width: 200px"}

A key feature of our design is the sliding attachment mechanism on the bottom surface. These sliders expand outward to grip different desk thicknesses, allowing the Desk Extender to fit a wide range of classroom desk styles. 
Once attached, a hinged secondary platform folds outward, providing an additional workspace area. We experimented with several hinge and lever-arm configurations to maximize stability, prevent wobbling, and ensure the extension felt secure during real use.

![Photo of old radio]({{ "/assets/images/DeskExtenderDemo.jpg" | relative_url }}){: .inline-image-l style="width: 200px"}

Both platforms use a hybrid material system of wood and acrylic. The wooden section provides strength and structure, while the acrylic surface serves as a functional mini–whiteboard that students can write on during lectures. To reach this final configuration, we tested different material combinations, slider geometries, and hinge placements through multiple prototyping cycles. 
Each iteration improved fit, comfort, and overall stability. This project strengthened my skills in collaborative design, prototyping, structural analysis, and user-centered engineering while producing a solution that meaningfully improves the everyday student experience.

![Photo of old radio]({{ "/assets/images/DeskExtenderDemo2.jpg" | relative_url }}){: .inline-image-l style="width: 200px"}

Overall, the Desk Extender showcased our ability to iterate quickly, prototype effectively, and deliver a practical, well-engineered product that genuinely improves the daily experience of students.
